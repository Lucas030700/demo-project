CORE PROCESS

Sales & Operations Planning

A cross-functional business planning process that aligns demand, supply, and financial plans into one achievable operating plan.

What it connects
  • Demand signals and commercial priorities
  • Supply, production, and capacity constraints
  • Inventory targets and financial outcomes
Why it matters

It creates one agreed plan so teams can make coordinated trade-offs instead of optimizing in silos.
